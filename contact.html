Read a summary of this message

Our volunteer coordinator welcomes you to Twin Cities Animal Rescue, thanks you for considering helping, and encourages you to fill out the form so the team can match you with a role that fits.

Service area

We serve Minneapolis, Saint Paul, and surrounding communities across the Twin Cities metro area, including Hennepin and Ramsey counties. Foster homes and volunteers within about 30 minutes of our office are the easiest to coordinate.

Office hours

  • Monday to Friday 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
  • Saturday (adoption days) 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
  • Sunday Closed

Hours are in Central Time. Visits by appointment are welcome.
