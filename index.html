Our mission

Rescue. Care. Connect.

Twin Cities Animal Rescue exists to save animals in need, give them the medical care and kindness they deserve, and connect them with loving families. We believe a stronger community makes a safer world for every animal in it.

Our work runs on volunteers, foster homes, and generous supporters. Every hour you give and every home you open changes a life.
